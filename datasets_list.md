# Datasets to use for Part 1

1. IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance
- Link: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/pavansubhasht/ibm-hr-analytics-attrition-dataset
- Description: 1,470 employee records with 34 attributes (age, monthly income, overtime, job satisfaction, years at company, distance from home, etc.) and a binary target `Attrition` (Yes/No) indicating whether the employee left the company. Used for supervised classification of employee turnover risk.

2. HR Analytics: Job Change of Data Scientists
- Link: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/arashnic/hr-analytics-job-change-of-data-scientists
- Description: 19,158 candidate records with 13 attributes (city development index, education level, experience, company size/type, training hours, etc.) and a binary target `target` indicating whether a trained candidate is looking for a job change. Used for supervised classification of candidate job-seeking intent.

Notes:
- These datasets complement a People Analytics role: IBM HR Attrition supports internal retention/attrition-risk modelling (who is likely to leave the company), while HR Job Change of Data Scientists supports talent-pipeline modelling (which trained candidates are likely to seek new employment). Together they give a People Analytics function both a retention lens and a hiring/talent-pipeline lens on workforce movement.
